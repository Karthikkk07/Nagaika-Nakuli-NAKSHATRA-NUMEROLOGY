## 5. Razorpay
- No official public list of prohibited businesses was found. Razorpay says approval depends on the exact activity; real-money gaming is the named prohibition. Astrotalk (astrology and numerology) and Jumaani (numerology) both use Razorpay, which shows such merchants are served, but it is no guarantee. Describe the services accurately and get written confirmation from Razorpay support before launch.
- Pages needed on the website (docs: razorpay.com/docs/payments/dashboard/account-settings/business-website-details.md): About us, Contact us, Pricing details, Terms and conditions, Privacy policy, Refund/cancellation policy. This site now has all of these except a dedicated "Pricing details" page (fees are on the Services page).
- KYC (razorpay.com/docs/payments/business-types-kyc-documents.md): individual or unregistered: PAN, mobile, identity and address (CKYC/DigiLocker or Aadhaar), bank account number and IFSC. Proprietorship: two business proofs (Udyam, GST, Shop and Establishment, etc.) plus owner PAN and ID. A personal bank account is allowed for unregistered individuals and sole proprietors; registered businesses need a current account.
