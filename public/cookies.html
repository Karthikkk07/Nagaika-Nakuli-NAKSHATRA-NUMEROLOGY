Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Cookies are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. We keep their use to a minimum.

NamePurposeKept for
googtrans cookieRemembers the language you pick in the “Read in” bar.Until you close your browser

Third parties

If an analytics service is switched on, it loads only after you press Accept in the cookie notice; it counts visits and sets Google Analytics cookies. If you pick a language other than English, the page is translated by Google Translate, which may set its own cookies. Videos from YouTube and payments through Razorpay load content from those companies, which may also use cookies under their own policies. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. The site still works, but your language choice will not be remembered.
