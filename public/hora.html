Hora Kala Chakram · హోర కాలచక్రం

In Vedic timekeeping each day is split into 24 horas: 12 between sunrise and sunset and 12 between sunset and the next sunrise. Each hora is ruled by a planet, and the first hora of the day belongs to the planet of that weekday. Because sunrise and sunset change with the place and the season, the timings below are worked out for your city.

Hora timings are calculated from the sunrise and sunset of the chosen place and may differ by a minute or two from a printed almanac (panchangam). They are traditional guidance for reflection, not a prediction or a guarantee. Green rows are the benefic planets (Moon, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus). The “day” here runs from sunrise to the next sunrise.
