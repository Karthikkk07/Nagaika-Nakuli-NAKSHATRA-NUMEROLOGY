Nadi Astrology and your fingerprint

Nadi astrology is a traditional system of reading in which a person’s life is studied from records that are matched to that person individually. The starting point is the fingerprint (thumb impression).

Every fingerprint is unique

No two people have the same fingerprint, not even identical twins. This is why the police and courts use fingerprints to tell one person from another, and why every person has a different one.

Why we ask for it

Because your fingerprint belongs only to you, it is the basis on which we check and match your reading. Once you send it, we study it and begin from your own print rather than from general details.

What happens next

You send a clear fingerprint and pay the fee. Our numerologist checks the fingerprint, then contacts you on the mobile number or email you give to discuss your reading.

How to send a good fingerprint

  1. Wash and dry your hands. Press your thumb (the right thumb for men and the left for women is the usual custom) on an ink pad, then press it firmly once on plain white paper without rolling or smudging.
  2. Take a clear, well-lit, straight-on photo of the print. Fill the frame with the print and avoid shadows and glare.
  3. Attach the photo in the form below. A phone camera is fine.
