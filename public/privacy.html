Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Nagaika, Nakuli Numerology (“we”, “us”), Bengaluru, respects your privacy. This page explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, how long we keep it and what rights you have, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, mobile number and email, when you send an enquiry, book a service or pay online.
  • Birth details: date, time and place of birth, and the names and birth details of a partner for marriage matching, only when you enter them in a form.
  • Fingerprint image: only for Nadi Astrology, and only when you upload it and tick the consent box.
  • Payment records: order number, amount, status and the payment reference from the payment gateway. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login. These are handled by the payment gateway.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse. We store a cookie to remember your chosen language (see the Cookie Policy).

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and deliver the service you asked for.
  • To process payments, send receipts and keep accounting records.
  • To confirm your mobile number with a one-time code where you choose to verify it.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising by other companies.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the site: the payment gateway (Razorpay), email and SMS providers, and the hosting provider. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order details for as long as needed to provide the service and for up to 3 years afterwards, or longer where the law requires (for example accounting records). Fingerprint images and birth details are deleted on request once the reading is complete.

5. How we protect it

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the admin area needs a password. Uploaded files are shown only to the administrator. No system is perfectly secure, so please share only what a service needs.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw a consent you gave. You may also name a person to act for you and may complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our reply. Write to anand@arninvest.in or call 9845610894. We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

7. Children

This site is meant for adults. A parent or guardian must give consent before data about a child under 18 is submitted.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
