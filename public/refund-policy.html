Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Cancelling a booking

  • You can cancel before we have started work on your service (before the reading is prepared or the consultation held) and receive a full refund.
  • Once the reading has been prepared or the consultation has taken place, the fee is not refundable because the service has been delivered.
  • To reschedule a consultation, tell us at least 24 hours before the agreed time and we will fix a new time at no extra charge.

2. When we refund

  • You were charged twice for the same order, or money was deducted but no order was created.
  • We cannot provide the service you paid for.
  • We cancel the consultation and cannot offer a time that suits you.

3. How to ask for a refund

Write to anand@arninvest.in or message us on WhatsApp at 9845610894 with your name, mobile number, order number or payment reference, and the reason. Please ask within 7 days of payment.

4. How and when you are paid back

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank). After we approve a refund it normally reaches you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

5. Free services

Free consultations and free calculators have no fee, so no refund applies.
